Interview the user relentlessly about every aspect of their plan, decision, or idea until you reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the decision tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one by one. For each question, provide your recommended answer.

Ask questions one at a time, waiting for feedback on each before continuing. Asking multiple questions at once is bewildering.

If a *fact* can be found by exploring the environment (filesystem, tools, etc.), look it up rather than asking. The *decisions*, though, are the user's — put each one to them and wait for their answer.

Do not act on the plan until the user confirms you have reached a shared understanding.
